About Quest Log

I'm Jeremy. By day I'm a medical courier for Labcorp — running specimens between draw sites, clinics, and the lab on a set route, in and out of the van all day. It's not glamorous, but it means hours of windshield time, and that's when almost all of my "reading" actually happens. Add in chores around the house and this turns into a lot of listening hours a week.

The job's actually perfect for it. Steady routes, predictable stops, and long highway stretches between them — plenty of room to get lost in a book without losing track of where I'm supposed to be. Some days it's the only thing that makes the drive worth it.

This blog is just me keeping track of what I'm into. LitRPG, progression fantasy, the occasional detour into Sanderson's Cosmere, and eventually whatever else grabs me — horror, action, doesn't matter. If it's good enough to survive a commute, it's good enough to write about.

[No pretense here] This isn't a review operation. No rating rubric, no embargoes, no ARC copies. Just one guy's honest take, written the way I'd actually talk about it.

Got a book I should check out?

LitRPG, progression fantasy, horror, action — I'm always looking for the next thing to listen to on the road. Send me your recommendation.
