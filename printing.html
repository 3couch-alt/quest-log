3D Printing: The Newest Side Quest

All this questing has landed me on 3D printing. Not totally sure how it happened — somewhere between the audiobooks and the game I'm building, a Bambu Lab P2S showed up on my desk, and now I can't stop making stuff I didn't know I needed.

Why this is here

Same reason the game dev page exists: this blog was always just me keeping track of what I'm into, and I'm apparently into this now too. No expertise to offer yet, no tutorials, just whatever I end up making and whatever I break figuring out how.
